**Orbit 1.3.0 beta 1: Qubit, and Orbit answers Claude for you.**

This is a beta. If you're on 1.2.0 you won't be offered it; install it by hand to try it. From then on, a beta install updates to later betas and to the next stable release.

- **Qubit,** a square with one eye, lives on your Claude window (Settings → Avatar: Pixel buddy, Qubit or None). He sleeps while Orbit sleeps, listens, thinks, talks with his eye as his mouth, shows feelings in time with his words, stomps Send, watches Claude work and celebrates when it's done. Ask him for a backflip, a roll or a jump. While nobody's talking to him he has a life of his own.
- **Pixel buddy:** a 16-pixel character along the bottom of the Claude window, if you'd rather.
- **"Hey Orbit" wake word** ("Hey Grok" still works).
- **Stop Claude by voice.** Say "stop" or "cancel that run" and Orbit presses Claude's own Stop button.
- **Answer Claude when it's blocked.** Permission prompts, Claude's questions and plans to approve: Orbit tells you, reads them, and answers with Claude's own buttons.
- **Drafts go straight into the box** as your own words, said to Claude. Ask for a change and the whole box is retyped. Sending still waits for your yes.
- **Web search** for both voice engines.
- **Claude voice (beta, opt-in):** Claude Haiku as the voice, through your own Claude Code sign-in, with speech heard and spoken on your PC. No xAI key needed.
- **Mute button**, and the orb can be turned off while an avatar is on.

You need Windows 10/11 and the Claude desktop app. With Grok as the voice you also need an [xAI API key](https://console.x.ai); voice is billed by xAI per minute while connected. The wake word costs nothing.

**Install:** download `Orbit-Setup-1.3.0-beta.1.exe` below and run it. It installs over 1.2.0. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so on the SmartScreen prompt click **More info → Run anyway**. You can verify it with the `.sha256` file.

The latest stable release is [1.2.0](https://github.com/Cookiebubba/orbit-voice/releases/tag/v1.2.0). See [CHANGELOG.md](../../blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for everything in this beta and [SECURITY.md](../../blob/main/SECURITY.md) for how Orbit protects your sessions, key and microphone.
